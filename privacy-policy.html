# Privacy Policy ## Color Sort Master — Pour. Sort. Conquer! **Effective Date:** October 18, 2026 Welcome to Color Sort Master. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information, what third-party services may collect, and how you can manage or delete your data. ### 1. Privacy Philosophy Color Sort Master is designed as an offline-first puzzle game. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play. Core gameplay progress, settings, and scores are stored locally on your device using AndroidX DataStore. Certain optional online features and third-party services may process information when enabled and when an internet connection is available. ### 2. Information We Collect **2.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** If advertisements are enabled in the game, Google Mobile Ads may collect or process information such as: - Advertising ID or other device identifiers, where permitted. - IP address and general location information derived from it. - Ad impressions, interactions, and advertising performance data. - Device and app information used for advertising, fraud prevention, and diagnostics. Google processes this information according to its own privacy policies and applicable settings. During development, test advertisements may be used. Players may purchase the **Remove Ads** option, if available, to disable standard in-game advertisements. This purchase does not necessarily disable data processing associated with other services. **2.2 Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Optional)** If Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics is integrated and enabled, these services may process: - Gameplay events, such as `level_started`, `level_completed`, and `hint_used`. - Device model, operating system version, and app version. - Crash reports, diagnostic traces, and app performance information. This information helps us understand gameplay performance, identify bugs, and improve the player experience. **2.3 Cloud Firestore (Optional)** If cloud synchronization is implemented and enabled, the game may store and synchronize gameplay information, including: - Unlocked levels and completed levels. - In-game coins and other game progression data. - An anonymous Firebase user identifier (UID). This anonymous profile is not designed to require your name, email address, or phone number. However, anonymous identifiers and associated technical information may still constitute personal data under applicable privacy laws. ### 3. Offline Gameplay The core puzzle gameplay is designed to work offline. Locally stored game data generally remains on your device unless you clear the app's data, uninstall the game, or use an available reset option. Online services, including advertisements, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud synchronization, may require an internet connection. ### 4. Children's Privacy and Families Policy Color Sort Master is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. If the game is distributed to children or participates in Google Play's Families program, applicable requirements for children's privacy, advertising, SDK usage, and age-appropriate content must be followed. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately may contact the developer to request assistance. ### 5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services We do not sell players' personal information as part of the game's intended operation. Information may be processed by third-party service providers when their SDKs or services are enabled, including: - **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** Advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. - **Firebase Analytics:** App usage and gameplay analytics. - **Firebase Crashlytics:** Crash reporting and diagnostics. - **Cloud Firestore:** Optional cloud storage and game progress synchronization. These providers process information under their applicable privacy policies and terms. Their data practices may change over time. ### 6. Data Retention and Deletion **Local game data:** You can reset your locally saved progress using **Settings → Reset Progress**, if this option is available. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game may also remove locally stored data. **Cloud game data:** If cloud synchronization is enabled, deleting local progress may not delete data already stored in Firebase. Cloud data deletion may require a separate deletion process. To request deletion of cloud-stored gameplay data associated with your anonymous profile, contact the developer using the contact details provided on the game's Google Play Store listing. Please include enough information to help identify the relevant game data without sending unnecessary personal information. Some technical records may be retained for a limited period where required for security, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes. ### 7. Advertising Choices and Consent Where applicable, players can manage advertising privacy preferences through available in-game consent controls or Google's advertising settings. Depending on the player's location and applicable laws, consent may be requested before certain advertising or analytics data is processed. Available controls may vary by region, device, and app version. Purchasing Remove Ads disables standard advertisements as described in the purchase terms, but does not automatically opt the player out of all analytics, diagnostics, or other data processing. ### 8. Data Security We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. The security of cloud-stored information also depends on the configuration of Firebase services, access controls, and the security measures implemented by the developer. ### 9. Your Privacy Rights Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise applicable rights, contact the developer using the support contact published on the game's Google Play Store listing. ### 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's features, third-party integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. ### 11.
