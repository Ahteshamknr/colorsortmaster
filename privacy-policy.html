# Privacy & Consent Policy ## Color Sort Master — Pour. Sort. Conquer! ### 1. Privacy Philosophy Color Sort Master is an offline-first puzzle game. It does not require players to create an account, provide names, email addresses, or phone numbers. All core gameplay progress, settings, and scores are stored locally on the device using AndroidX DataStore. ### 2. Information Collected Automatically When network connectivity is active and optional third-party SDKs are enabled: 1. **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: - Collects standard Android Advertising ID to serve ads. - Test ads are used during development. - Players can disable standard advertisements at any time by purchasing the "Remove Ads" option. 2. **Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Optional)**: - Aggregated event counts (e.g. `level_started`, `level_completed`, `hint_used`). - Device model, OS version, and diagnostic crash traces. 3. **Cloud Firestore (Optional)**: - Synchronizes player progression (`unlockedLevel`, `completedLevels`, `coins`) linked solely to an anonymous Firebase UID. - No personal identity is ever associated with the anonymous player profile. ### 3. COPPA & Family Policy Compliance - Color Sort Master is suitable for general audiences. - Does not intentionally collect personal data from children under 13. - In-game advertisements comply with Google Play Families Policy guidelines. ### 4. Player Control & Data Deletion - Players can reset all locally saved game progress at any time via **Settings -> Reset Progress**. - To request deletion of anonymous cloud progress, players can reset their data or contact developer support.
