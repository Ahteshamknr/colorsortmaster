Privacy Policy

Color Sort Master — Pour. Sort. Conquer!

Your privacy matters to us
Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Welcome to Color Sort Master. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information, which third-party services may process data, and the choices available to you.

01. Privacy Philosophy

Color Sort Master is designed as an offline-first puzzle game. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play.

Core gameplay progress, settings, and scores are stored locally on your device using AndroidX DataStore. Optional online features and third-party services may process information when enabled and when an internet connection is available.

02. Information Collected

2.1 Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

If advertisements are enabled, Google Mobile Ads may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers, where permitted.
  • IP address and general location information derived from it.
  • Advertisement impressions, interactions, and performance data.
  • Device and app information used for advertising, fraud prevention, and diagnostics.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and applicable settings. Test advertisements may be used during development.

If available, the Remove Ads purchase disables standard in-game advertisements. It does not necessarily disable data processing by other services.

2.2 Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Optional)

If integrated and enabled, these services may process:

  • Gameplay events such as level_started, level_completed, and hint_used.
  • Device model, operating system version, and app version.
  • Crash reports, diagnostic traces, and app performance information.

This information helps identify technical problems and improve the player experience.

2.3 Cloud Firestore (Optional)

If cloud synchronization is implemented and enabled, the game may store and synchronize:

  • Unlocked and completed levels.
  • In-game coins and other game progression data.
  • An anonymous Firebase user identifier (UID).

The anonymous profile is not designed to require your name, email address, or phone number. Anonymous identifiers and associated technical information may nevertheless qualify as personal data under applicable laws.

03. Offline Gameplay

Core puzzle gameplay is designed to work offline. Locally stored data generally remains on your device unless you clear app storage, uninstall the game, or use an available reset option.

Online services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud synchronization may require an internet connection.

04. Children's Privacy

Color Sort Master is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13.

If the game is distributed to children or participates in Google Play's Families program, applicable requirements for children's privacy, advertising, SDK usage, and age-appropriate content must be followed.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has inappropriately provided personal information may contact the developer for assistance.

05. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell players' personal information as part of the game's intended operation.

When enabled, third-party services may process information for:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Firebase Analytics: App usage and gameplay analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting and diagnostics.
  • Cloud Firestore: Optional cloud storage and progress synchronization.

These providers handle information under their respective privacy policies and terms.

06. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data

You can reset locally saved progress through Settings → Reset Progress, if available. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game may also remove locally stored information.

Cloud game data

Resetting local progress may not delete information already stored in Firebase. Cloud data may require a separate deletion request.

To request deletion of cloud-stored gameplay data associated with your anonymous profile, contact the developer using the support contact published on the game's Google Play Store listing. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Certain technical records may be retained for a limited period when required for security, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

07. Advertising Choices and Consent

Where available, players can manage advertising privacy preferences through in-game consent controls or Google's advertising settings.

Depending on your location and applicable law, consent may be required before certain advertising or analytics data is processed. Available controls may vary by region, device, and app version.

Important: Purchasing Remove Ads disables standard advertisements as described in the purchase terms. It does not automatically disable all analytics, diagnostics, or other data processing.

08. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the game. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Cloud data security also depends on Firebase configuration, access controls, and the security measures implemented by the developer.

09. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise applicable rights, contact the developer using the support contact published on the game's Google Play Store listing.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when game features, third-party integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other concerns about Color Sort Master, contact the developer through the support contact provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
